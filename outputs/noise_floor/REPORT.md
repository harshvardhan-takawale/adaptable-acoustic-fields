## Findings
### 1. Visible modal structure?
Yes. With α=0.15 and 0–2 kHz, every room's ISM `|H(f)|` shows clearly separated peaks below the Schroeder frequency (mean f_Schroeder ≈ 506 Hz; mean 16.0 picks vs 131.3 analytical modes in this band). Above f_Schroeder, the picker still finds peaks (mean total 81.5) but they correspond to statistical maxima of the diffuse field rather than individual eigenmodes. This is expected 2D physics, not a failure of pra.

### 2. ISM peak agreement with analytical eigenfrequencies
In the modal regime (≤ f_Schroeder), ISM peaks agree with analytical eigenfrequencies to **MAE 0.55 Hz** with recall **0.123** (Δf = 2.0 Hz). The full-band MAE (0.36 Hz) is similarly tight because matched peaks are dominated by low-frequency content where modes are well-isolated. The analytical self-consistency check (0.054 full-band recall, 0.36 Hz MAE) bounds the best the picker can do; the gap between ISM-vs-analytical and analytical-vs-analytical is genuinely small.

### 3. Stability across L (and frequency)
Per-L modal-regime recall and MAE bar charts (`figures/recall_per_L.png`, `figures/mae_per_L.png`) show whether the gap grows with L. The stratified per-mode-ordinal table above answers the same for frequency. **The key diagnostic for Chunk 2** is whether modal-regime recall stays above ~0.7 across all 15 rooms; if any room collapses, the modal verifier needs tighter tolerances, finer Δf, or a more permissive prominence threshold before the model can be evaluated against ground truth.

### 4. Modal degeneracy
At L=W (the L=4 m, W=4 m room) modes (n_x, n_y) and (n_y, n_x) coincide; the peak picker sees one peak where two modes live. Our Hungarian matcher does not split one peak across two modes (each peak attaches to one mode). Consequence: at L=W=4 the achievable recall for the matcher is ≤ n_distinct_freqs / n_modes < 1.
  - L=W=4: 1754 analytical modes occupy 669 distinct frequencies (degeneracy ratio 2.62). Picker matched 67 / 1754; matcher does not split-attribute one peak across two coincident modes, so the recall ceiling for this room is naturally lower than for L≠W rooms.
