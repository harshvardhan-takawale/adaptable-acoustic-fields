**Spatial mode shapes at L = 5.25 m (unseen).** Each of the 6 panels shows the
analytical pressure magnitude (ISM, left) next to the model's predicted
magnitude (right), evaluated on the 8×8 receiver grid at the corresponding
eigenfrequency. Color scale is per-panel-normalised dB (-40 to 0 dB; 0 dB =
per-mode peak), so the geometry of each mode is directly comparable
side-by-side even when the absolute amplitudes differ.

The model recovers the spatial mode structure of each of the first 6 modes —
per-panel correlation values (shown in the panel titles) range from 0.85 to
0.99 at this L. The amplitudes don't match exactly (the spatial Pearson
correlation we use is invariant to a global complex scale, by design); shape
geometry does. This is the strongest single-L visualisation of the V0/V1
spatial-alignment result.
